## Overview

This is an integrated app combining three apps: [NamaMetro](/Store/NamaMeto/NamaMetoStore.html) (metronome), [NamaTune](/Store/NamaChu/NamaChuStore.html) (tuner), and [NamaHam](/Store/NamaHam/NamaHamStore.html) (harmony).

NamaSound+ itself is just an outer container; the contents open as each individual app.

### How to Use
Press the tabs at the top to switch between the three apps.

You can change the language and sleep prevention settings for all three apps at once by going to "Settings" in the upper left. You can also change these settings individually within each app.

### Settings
Settings to stop sound when switching tabs, adjust display size, force reload, install as PWA, and copy URLs to open each app directly.

### Screen Explanation (How to read screenshots)
1) Metronome tab: NamaMetro
2) Tuner tab: NamaTune
3) Harmony tab: NamaHam
4) Common settings: Settings for the integrated application, opened from the gear icon in the upper left.
